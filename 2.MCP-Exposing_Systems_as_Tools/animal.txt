Wild Animals

Animal: Jaguar
Habitat: Amazon rainforest
Diet: Carnivore
Fact: Jaguars are powerful predators and are excellent swimmers.

Animal: Sloth
Habitat: Tropical forests of Central and South America
Diet: Leaves
Fact: Sloths move slowly and spend much of their time in trees.

Animal: Anaconda
Habitat: Rivers, wetlands and forests of South America
Diet: Carnivore
Fact: Green anacondas are among the largest snakes in the world.

Animal: Harpy Eagle
Habitat: Tropical forests of Central and South America
Diet: Carnivore
Fact: The harpy eagle is a powerful forest-dwelling bird of prey.
